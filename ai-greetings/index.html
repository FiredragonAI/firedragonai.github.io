它做什么

  • 春节、中秋、圣诞、感恩节……中西节日和每个人的生日(阳历或农历)到了,提前 3 天提醒你
  • 按每个人的称谓、关系、近况写祝福,一人一版,不是群发
  • 中文、英文或中英双语;23 套节日配图
  • 一键分享到微信、WhatsApp、iMessage

Reminds you before every holiday and birthday (solar or lunar), writes a personal greeting for each person based on how you know them, in Chinese, English or both, with a matching card — then shares to WeChat, WhatsApp or iMessage in one tap.

安装步骤 · How to install

  1. 点上面的按钮下载 ai-greetings.apk
  2. 打开下载的文件;系统提示「允许安装未知来源应用」时选允许(只对本次生效)
  3. 安装完成后打开,先加 5 个最重要的人,或从通讯录导入

Tap the button, open the downloaded APK, allow "install from this source" when asked, then add your 5 closest people or import from contacts.

隐私 · Privacy

联系人和发送历史只存在你的手机里。生成祝福时只把当次需要的称谓、关系、备注发给服务器,不上传通讯录,不用于训练。没网也能用本地模板生成。

People and history stay on your phone. Only the fields needed for one greeting are sent when generating; your address book is never uploaded or used for training. Works offline with local templates.
